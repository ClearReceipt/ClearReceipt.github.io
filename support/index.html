Frequently asked questions

The total or line items were read incorrectly. What can I do?

Text recognition works best on flat, well-lit receipts photographed straight on, with the whole receipt in frame. Before saving, you can tap the total or any line item to correct it. Always double-check amounts you rely on for expenses or taxes.

Can I add a receipt without a photo?

Yes. Tap +, switch to Manual, and enter the store, category, and line items yourself.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, and CHF. Set your default in Settings › Default Currency, or choose a currency per receipt.

Where are my receipts stored? Are they backed up?

Only on your iPhone. ClearReceipt has no account or cloud sync. Your receipts are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have one, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along. If you delete the app without a backup, your receipts are gone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a single receipt from its detail screen. To remove everything, open Settings › Erase All Data. This deletes every receipt, all stored images, and resets your preferences. It can't be undone. Clear Stored Images removes only the photos and keeps your receipt details.

Why does the app show ads? Why was I asked about tracking?

Ads keep ClearReceipt free. iOS asks whether the app may use your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads; the app works the same whichever you choose. You can change it anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The app can't access my camera or photos.

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to ClearReceipt, and turn on Camera and Photos access.

What do I need to run ClearReceipt?

An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
