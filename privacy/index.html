Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version: your receipts, photos, and amounts stay on your iPhone. We don't run a server, we don't have accounts, and we never see your receipts. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve them. You control ad tracking with the prompts the app shows you.

This policy explains how the ClearReceipt iOS app (“ClearReceipt”, “the app”) handles information. ClearReceipt is developed by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”). If you have questions, contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you put into ClearReceipt is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Receipt details: store or merchant name, date, total, currency, category, line items, and notes.
  • Receipt images: photos you take or import of your receipts.
  • Preferences: settings such as your default currency.

Text recognition (reading the text on a receipt) is performed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Receipt images and their contents are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

We do not have access to this information. If your device is set up to back up with iCloud or to a computer, Apple includes app data in those backups under Apple's own privacy terms.

2. Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access so you can photograph receipts, and photo library access so you can import an existing receipt photo. The app only reads the photo you choose and never changes or deletes anything in your photo library. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ClearReceipt is free and supported by banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

DataDetails
Device identifiersThe Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and other app or device identifiers
Device & network informationDevice model, operating system, language, and IP address
Approximate locationGeneral location derived from your IP address (not GPS)

Google processes this information under its own policies. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

This information is never combined with your receipts, which stay on your device.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Tracking permission: iOS asks whether ClearReceipt may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. Without consent, Google may show limited or non-personalized ads.
  • US state privacy rights: where applicable, you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising using the privacy options shown in the app.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or collect your name, email, or contact details through the app.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services beyond what Apple provides to developers in anonymized, aggregated form if you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers.
  • We don't sell your receipt data. We never receive it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your receipts stay on your device until you delete them. You can:

  • delete an individual receipt from its detail screen;
  • delete all stored receipt images with Settings › Clear Stored Images;
  • delete every receipt, image, and preference with Settings › Erase All Data;
  • delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state laws such as the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because your receipt data is only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app as described above. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy, or contact us and we'll help where we can.

8. Children

ClearReceipt is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or on this page.
