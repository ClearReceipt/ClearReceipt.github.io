1. What is this Privacy Policy and what does it cover?

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the ClearReceipt app for iPhone (“ClearReceipt” or “the app”), how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. ClearReceipt is developed by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”, “our”).

It covers the app, this website, and messages you send to our support address. It does not cover Apple's App Store, iOS, or Google's services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

ClearReceipt does not have user accounts. You never sign up, sign in, or give us your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

2. What information do we collect?

Information you create in the app (stays on your device)

When you use ClearReceipt, you create information that is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

  • Receipt details: store or merchant name, date, total, currency, category, line items (description, quantity, price), and notes.
  • Receipt images: photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library.
  • Text read from receipts: the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text from your receipt images. This happens on your iPhone, and images and text are not uploaded.
  • Preferences: settings such as your default currency.

Information from your device's permissions

  • Camera: used only while you are photographing a receipt.
  • Photo library: used only to read the single photo you choose to import. The app never adds, changes, or deletes anything in your photo library.

The app does not request your precise location, contacts, microphone, health data, or other device permissions.

Information collected by our advertising partner

The app displays banner ads from Google AdMob. Google's software in the app may collect the following information when ads are shown:

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to attach, such as a screenshot or receipt photo.

Information from Apple

Apple provides developers with aggregated App Store and app usage statistics, and with crash reports from users who have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings. This information does not identify you to us.

3. How do we use your information?

  • To provide the app's features: on your device, the app uses your receipt information to read receipts, show and search your list, filter by date, and calculate totals. This happens only on your device.
  • To show ads: Google uses the information described above to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and, only where you allow it, personalize ads.
  • To prevent fraud and keep advertising safe: Google uses device and ad information to detect invalid traffic and abuse.
  • To provide support: we use the information you send us to answer your questions and fix problems.
  • To improve the app: we use aggregated statistics and crash reports from Apple, and feedback you send us, to fix bugs and improve features.

We do not use your receipts for advertising, and we never combine information from Google with your receipts. We don't have your receipts.

4. How is your information shared with third parties?

Your receipts and receipt images are never shared by the app. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Advertising partner: Google receives the information described in section 2 directly from the app to serve and measure ads. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Apple: Apple operates the App Store and provides iOS features such as iCloud Backup. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, your device backups include the app's data under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Email provider: support messages you send are stored by our email provider.
  • Legal and safety reasons: as described in section 9.
  • Change of ownership: if the app is sold or transferred, information we hold (such as support emails) may be transferred to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money. See section 14 for how US state laws treat advertising-related sharing.

5. Identifiers and similar technologies

The app does not use browser cookies. Google's advertising software uses device identifiers and local storage on your device, which work similarly to cookies, to deliver and measure ads and remember your consent choices.

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA): available to Google only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
  • Consent records: your ad consent choices are stored on your device so you aren't asked repeatedly.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. How can you manage or delete your information and exercise your rights?

Your receipts

Because your receipts exist only on your device, you control them directly:

  • View and edit: open any receipt to see or change its details.
  • Delete one receipt: use Delete on the receipt's detail screen.
  • Delete receipt images only: Settings › Clear Stored Images.
  • Delete everything: Settings › Erase All Data removes every receipt, all images, and your preferences.
  • Remove the app: deleting ClearReceipt removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your device backups are managed through Apple.

Advertising choices

  • Tracking: choose “Ask App Not to Track” when prompted, or change it later in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent: where required, the app asks for your consent to personalized ads and lets you change your choice later.

Device permissions

Turn camera or photo access on or off at any time in iOS Settings › ClearReceipt.

Information you sent us

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your support emails by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will respond within the time required by law, generally 30 days.

7. How long do we keep your information?

  • Receipts, images, and preferences: on your device until you delete them, erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Support emails: up to two years after our last exchange, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we must keep them longer by law.
  • Advertising information: retained by Google under its own retention policies; see How Google retains data.

8. How is information transferred internationally?

Your receipts are not transferred anywhere; they stay on your device. Advertising information collected by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google relies on legal mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Support emails may be stored by our email provider outside your country.

9. How do we respond to legal requests and prevent harm?

We may access, keep, or disclose information we hold (such as support emails) if we believe in good faith it is required by law, for example in response to a valid court order, or necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, us, or others.

Because your receipts are stored only on your device and we never receive them, we cannot provide them to anyone, including in response to a legal request.

10. How is your information protected?

Your receipt information is stored in the app's sandbox, which other apps cannot access, and is protected by iOS data protection when your device is locked with a passcode. Keep your device passcode secure and your software up to date. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit risk by not collecting your receipts at all.

11. Children's privacy

ClearReceipt is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. How will you know the policy has changed?

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. If changes are significant, we will also notify you in the app or in the App Store release notes before they take effect.
